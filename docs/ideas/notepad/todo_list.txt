0- **Attendance intention:** A member indicates their intention to attend an event. This does not count as confirmed attendance; a coordinator must confirm the member’s presence. This feature is useful to the whole group easily understand who and in what position is attending an event.
---
For example, Missa:
Leitor 1: Eduardo
Leitor 2: Mariana
Salmo: 
Banda: Enzo, João, Giulia, Livia
---
These are all presence intentions, but not confirmed, since the event has not begun. Now the whole group can see that Leitor 1 and Leitor 2 are filled, but Salmo is not. Therefore, a member can express intention of attending the Missa as Salmo reader.
